## LLM routing gateways: billing caps as the overlooked feature

Hard billing caps — a maximum spend limit across all API calls — are largely absent from direct provider offerings but are the feature practitioners cite most when justifying an LLM routing gateway like [OpenRouter](../tools/openrouter.md). For public-facing agent deployments at risk of abuse, the absence of a cap is a meaningful operational risk, and the ~5% gateway surcharge can be the cheaper option.

The broader tradeoff: the surcharge is easily justified during exploration, when you're testing many models and switching frequently. It's harder to justify at high volume on a fixed production model set where the routing flexibility goes unused.

Token-volume leaderboards on routing platforms are structurally unreliable for model selection — they show aggregate volume without unique-user counts, so a single high-volume application can dominate the rankings.
